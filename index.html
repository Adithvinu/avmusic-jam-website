About Me

♪

I am Adith Vinu & I am an upcoming college student at the University of Texas at Dallas with over a decade of experience playing piano and exploring music technology. I started learning the piano at age 6 and over time, I have performed at recitals, cultural events, attained certifications from Trinity College London for Digital Keyboard and I have composed music for short films.

Growing up, I had to navigate learning these instruments and technologies largely on my own, which is why I created AVMusic&Jam: to give students a more guided, engaging, and effective path to understanding music. Every student learns differently, therefore, every lesson will be personalized to their pace, goals, and musical interests.

What You Will Learn

The Curriculum

𝄞
Topic What You'll Learn Skills Gained
🎹Piano Foundations Proper posture, hand position, and finger numbering; navigating the keyboard Physical technique, comfort at the keys
🎵Note Reading Basics Treble & bass clef notes, staff reading, simple rhythms (quarter, half, whole notes) Sheet music literacy, rhythm accuracy
🔑Major Scales & Chords C, G, D, F major scales; basic triads (I, IV, V chords) Finger dexterity, foundational harmony
👂Ear Training Intro Identifying high vs. low pitches, simple melodies by ear, clapping rhythms Ear-to-hand coordination, musical awareness
🎶First Songs Simple pieces from Pop, Worship, and Classical genres using both hands Song performance, musical expression
📖Basic Music Theory What is a beat? Measures, time signatures (4/4, 3/4), dynamics (loud/soft) Music comprehension, reading confidence
Topic What You'll Learn Skills Gained
🎹Advanced Technique Scales in all 12 keys, arpeggios, chord inversions, pedal technique Fluency, dynamic range, expressive playing
🎵Chord Progressions I–IV–V–vi in multiple keys; 7th chords; common pop & worship progressions Harmonic vocabulary, song understanding
👂Play By Ear Transcribing simple melodies; identifying chord progressions by sound; interval training Aural skills, musical independence
💡Intro to Improvisation Pentatonic scale improvisation, call-and-response patterns, melodic variation Creativity, musical instinct
🎶Genre Exploration Playing pieces across Classical, Pop, Gospel/Worship, and R&B styles Stylistic versatility, performance confidence
📖Music Theory II Key signatures, modes introduction, reading lead sheets, basic sight-reading Deeper music literacy, arrangement ability
Topic What You'll Learn Skills Gained
🎹Performance Mastery Complex pieces with varied dynamics, rubato, pedaling nuance, and stage presence Concert-level performance, emotional depth
🎵Advanced Harmony Extended chords (9th, 11th, 13th), borrowed chords, modulations, reharmonization Jazz/neo-soul vocabulary, sophisticated arranging
💡Advanced Improvisation Modal improvisation, blues & jazz scales, improvising over chord changes Real-time musical conversation, stage confidence
🎧Music Technology DAW basics, MIDI recording, digital keyboards, production workflow intro Studio skills, modern music creation
✍️Composition & Arranging Writing original melodies, harmonizing tunes, arranging songs for piano Creative output, personal musical voice
📖Music Theory III Form analysis, counterpoint basics, score reading, ear training for complex intervals Scholarly musicianship, professional-level literacy
