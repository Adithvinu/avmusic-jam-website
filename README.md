# avmusic-jam-website
I am Adith Vinu & I am a music enthusiast with over a decade of experience playing piano and exploring music technology. 
I started learning the piano at age 6 and over time, I have performed at recitals, cultural events, attained certifications from Trinity College London for Digital Keyboard and I have composed music for short films.
Growing up, I had to navigate learning these instruments and technologies largely on my own, which is why I created AVMusic&Jam: to give students a more guided, engaging, and effective path to understanding music.
Every student learns differently, therefore, every lesson will be personalized to their pace, goals, and musical interests.
