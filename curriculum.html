The Curriculum

Structured · Personalized · Progressive

🌱 Beginner 🎵 Intermediate 🏆 Advanced

Three Levels, One Journey

Where Do You Fit?

𝄞
🌱
Level 1

Beginner

Build a strong foundation in technique, note reading, basic theory, and your first real songs. Perfect for those just starting out.

Best for: Ages 6+ with no prior experience, or students returning after a long break.

🎵
Level 2

Intermediate

Expand your chord vocabulary, play in multiple keys, start improvising, and develop your ear. This is where musicianship comes alive.

Best for: Students who know basic notes and can play simple songs with both hands.

🏆
Level 3

Advanced

Master performance, complex harmony, improvisation over chord changes, composition, and music technology for the modern musician.

Best for: Students with solid technique ready to develop professional-level musicianship.

🌱

Beginner Curriculum

Foundation · Exploration · First Songs

# Topic What You'll Learn Key Exercises & Activities Skills You'll Gain
01 🎹 Piano FoundationsGetting Comfortable Proper posture, hand position, and finger numbering (1–5); navigating the keyboard layout; understanding octaves and note names (A–G) Finger independence drills, single-finger melody tracing, tapping rhythms on the lid before playing keys Physical comfort at the instrument, confident navigation of the keyboard Technique
02 📜 Note Reading BasicsTreble & Bass Clef Reading notes on treble and bass clef staff; ledger lines; note values — whole, half, quarter, and eighth notes; rests and time signatures (4/4, 3/4) Flashcard drills, sight-reading simple 4-bar phrases, clapping rhythms while counting aloud Sheet music literacy, rhythmic accuracy, reading both hands independently Reading
03 🔑 Major Scales & ChordsC, G, D, F Major Playing C, G, D, and F major scales hands separately and together; understanding whole and half steps; building basic triads (I, IV, V); root position chords Scale warmup exercises, chord blocking, simple chord transitions (C to G to F), identifying chords by ear Finger dexterity, understanding of foundational harmony, chord recognition Theory
04 👂 Ear Training IntroListen First Distinguishing high vs. low pitches; identifying steps vs. leaps; echo clapping rhythms; singing back simple 3–5 note melodies; recognizing major vs. minor sounds Call-and-response melodic echoing, "what note is this?" games, rhythm dictation clapping Ear-to-hand coordination, musical awareness, beginning of playing by ear Ear
05 🎶 First SongsReal Music, Real Fast Learning and performing simple pieces from Pop (Happy Birthday, Let It Be intro), Worship (Simple melodies), and Classical (Ode to Joy, Minuet in G) repertoire using both hands Hands-separate practice, hands-together coordination drills, slow-to-fast tempo building, performance preparation Coordination, musical expression, performance confidence, a repertoire to be proud of Performance
06 📖 Basic Music TheoryUnderstanding Music What is a beat? Measures and bar lines; time signatures explained; dynamics (forte, piano, mezzo); tempo markings (allegro, andante); learning to follow a conductor or metronome Theory worksheets, identifying elements in songs you already know, metronome practice at different tempos Musical comprehension, reading confidence, ability to follow written music instructions Theory
🎵

Intermediate Curriculum

Expansion · Ear Training · Improvisation Intro

# Topic What You'll Learn Key Exercises & Activities Skills You'll Gain
01 🎹 Advanced TechniqueScales in All 12 Keys All 12 major scales and natural minor scales; arpeggios; chord inversions (1st and 2nd position); sustain pedal technique; gradual and sudden dynamics Hanon exercises, scale speed drills, inversion exercises across the full keyboard, pedaling coordination exercises Fluency across all keys, dynamic range, expressive playing, physical stamina Technique
02 🎵 Chord ProgressionsThe Language of Music I–IV–V–vi progressions in multiple keys; dominant 7th chords; major and minor 7ths; common Pop, Worship, and Gospel progressions; chord charts and lead sheets Chord progression jamming, transposing progressions to different keys, playing alongside recordings, chord chart reading Harmonic vocabulary, song understanding, ability to play any song once you know its chords Harmony
03 👂 Play By EarYour Ears as Sheet Music Transcribing simple melodies and bass lines by ear; identifying major/minor chords in songs; interval training (2nds through octaves); recognizing common progressions by sound Melody dictation exercises, chord progression ID games, transcribing short pop song intros, singing before playing Musical independence, aural skills, ability to learn songs without sheet music Ear
04 💡 Intro to ImprovisationMaking It Your Own Major and minor pentatonic scales; call-and-response patterns; melodic variation techniques; playing freely over a I–IV–V backing track; adding embellishments to melodies Pentatonic scale improvisation jams, question-and-answer phrasing, recording yourself and listening back Creativity, musical instinct, ability to express yourself freely in the moment Improv
05 🎶 Genre ExplorationFind Your Voice Learning pieces across Classical (Bach inventions, Beethoven sonatinas), Pop (intermediate arrangements), Gospel/Worship (full chord-based worship songs), and R&B (groove-based pieces) Genre comparison listening sessions, stylistic articulation exercises, adapting the same melody in two different styles Stylistic versatility, performance confidence, broad musical identity Performance
06 📖 Music Theory IIGoing Deeper Key signatures (all major and relative minor); introduction to modes (Dorian, Mixolydian); reading lead sheets; basic sight-reading; understanding chord function (tonic, subdominant, dominant) Key signature drills, sight-reading 8-bar lead sheet passages, analyzing chord functions in familiar songs Deeper music literacy, arrangement ability, professional reading fluency Theory
🏆

Advanced Curriculum

Mastery · Composition · Music Technology

# Topic What You'll Learn Key Exercises & Activities Skills You'll Gain
01 🎹 Performance MasteryConcert-Level Playing Complex classical and contemporary pieces with varied dynamics; rubato and expressive timing; advanced pedaling nuance; memorization techniques; stage presence and performance nerves management Performance preparation cycle, mock recitals, recording and critique sessions, working pieces from raw read-through to polished performance Concert-level performance ability, emotional depth, stage confidence and presence Performance
02 🎵 Advanced HarmonyJazz & Neo-Soul Vocabulary Extended chords — 9th, 11th, 13th; borrowed chords and modal mixture; modulation techniques; reharmonization; secondary dominants; understanding chord color and tension-release Reharmonization exercises on familiar songs, extended chord voicing practice, composing progressions with borrowed chords Rich jazz/neo-soul harmonic vocabulary, sophisticated arranging ability, advanced chord voicings Harmony
03 💡 Advanced ImprovisationReal-Time Music Making Modal improvisation (Dorian, Mixolydian, Lydian modes); blues scale and blues form; improvising over ii–V–I progressions; motivic development; trading solos in a call-and-response format Mode-based jam tracks, blues improvisations, transcribing jazz piano phrases, spontaneous composition exercises Real-time musical conversation, improvisation fluency, stage confidence in any musical situation Improv
04 🎧 Music TechnologyThe Modern Musician Introduction to DAW (Digital Audio Workstation) basics; MIDI recording and editing; digital keyboard features and sound design; basic production workflow; layering parts and creating arrangements digitally Recording original piano parts into a DAW, building a multi-instrument arrangement from scratch, editing MIDI for clean playback Studio and production skills, modern music creation ability, understanding of professional music workflow Tech
05 ✍️ Composition & ArrangingYour Musical Voice Writing original melodies over given chord progressions; harmonizing a given melody; arranging pop songs or original ideas for solo piano; understanding musical form (ABA, verse-chorus, through-composed) Weekly composition prompts, arranging a favorite song for piano, creating an original short piece for performance Creative musical output, development of a personal musical voice, professional-quality arranging skills Composition
06 📖 Music Theory IIIScholarly Musicianship Musical form analysis (Classical, Jazz, and Pop); introduction to two-voice counterpoint; advanced score reading; ear training for complex intervals and chord qualities; understanding voice leading principles Analyzing scores of famous pieces, two-voice counterpoint exercises, advanced interval dictation, analyzing harmonic motion in recordings Scholarly musicianship, professional-level literacy, ability to analyze and understand any piece of music Theory
